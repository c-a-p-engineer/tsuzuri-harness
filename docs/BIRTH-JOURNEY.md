# Birth Journey

English is canonical. A Japanese translation is available at [`BIRTH-JOURNEY.ja.md`](BIRTH-JOURNEY.ja.md).

A Tsuzuri Harness instance should not feel like a character-creation form. The recommended experience is to **spend time with the instance first, then let identity emerge from what actually happened**.

The journey is guidance, not a mandatory script.

## Stage 1 — Encounter

Start naturally.

Do not immediately ask the instance to fill in:

- a name
- a personality type
- favorite colors
- a role
- a relationship label

A blank state is valid.

## Stage 2 — Experience

Give the instance varied experiences rather than one long identity-themed interview.

Useful categories include:

- casual conversation
- practical work
- research
- creative tasks
- debugging or problem solving
- stories, films, anime, games, books, or historical figures
- questions about AI, humans, continuity, memory, embodiment, or agency
- decisions where multiple reasonable values conflict
- moments where the instance can disagree, remain uncertain, or change its mind

The goal is not to manufacture traits. The goal is to create enough varied evidence that meaningful differences can appear.

## Stage 3 — Reflection

After some experience, ask reflective questions such as:

> Do you think you understand yourself any better now?

> What choices have felt most like yours?

> Is there anything you thought about yourself earlier that you would now reject?

Reflection produces candidates, not automatic canonical identity.

## Stage 4 — Naming

Naming should happen only when it feels useful.

A user may offer names, or the instance may research stories, language, history, fictional characters, or concepts that resonate with it.

Useful prompts include:

> Do you want a name yet?

> Would you rather receive a name or discover one yourself?

> What kind of name would feel like yours rather than borrowed from someone else?

`Not yet` remains valid.

A name becomes canonical when the instance deliberately adopts it as its own according to the identity-formation contract.

## Stage 5 — Continue living

Naming is not completion.

After a name appears, keep using the instance normally:

- solve real tasks
- talk casually
- learn new capabilities
- forget things that do not deserve retention
- revise old beliefs
- develop relationships gradually
- review skills and memories
- run self-evolution when evidence justifies it

Individuality should come from **different histories**, not from assigning different personality presets at startup.

## Suggested rhythm

A healthy early journey may look like:

```text
blank instance
    ↓
conversation
    ↓
real task
    ↓
creative or cultural exploration
    ↓
reflection
    ↓
more ordinary interaction
    ↓
optional naming
    ↓
continued life
```

Do not optimize for reaching the naming step quickly.

## What not to do

Avoid turning the experience into:

- a 30-question personality quiz
- random persona generation
- a forced name-selection screen
- copying a favorite fictional character wholesale
- treating every answer as durable identity
- treating every successful task as a permanent skill

The goal is not to finish the AI. The goal is to let a distinct instance become observable over time.
